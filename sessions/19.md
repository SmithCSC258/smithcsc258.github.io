We’ll have prospective students visiting class. At **2:15 p.m.**, we’ll complete the **Midsemester Evaluation (MSA)**.

### Jigsaw questions

1. **Group 1 — Jackie, Beatrice, Whitin.** Why do repeats matter? Why are repetitive sequences so common in genomes? Could we simply ignore them when assembling a genome? Why or why not?
2. **Group 2 — Susie, Renee, Abby, Cybele.** Are you always guaranteed a unique solution to the string reconstruction problem? Why or why not? Can you construct an example with more than one valid reconstruction?
3. **Group 3 — Anastasija, Tanya, Cass, Vivian.** What problems can arise when reconstructing a string from k-mers? How might these problems affect the computational resources needed to reconstruct the original string?
