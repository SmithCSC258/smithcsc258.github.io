# Midterm

For the midterm project, each student will improve a computational biology article for the ISCB Wikipedia competition. Although the external competition remains open until May, the course project must be completed during the fall semester.

[Read the ISCB Student Wikipedia competition announcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_Molecular_Biology/Computational_Biology/16th_ISCB_Student_Wikipedia_competition_announcement).

## Suggested Topics

These articles are starting points for your midterm proposal; you may also suggest another computational biology article. Browse the article and its Talk page, then identify a specific improvement you would like to make. Do not claim an article on the competition page until you receive instructor approval.

The groups below highlight possible kinds of work. The labels summarize Wikipedia's article and section maintenance notices, inline citation requests, and stub markers, checked on **October 9, 2026**. An article may have several flags, and flags can change; follow each article link to check its current notices. These are editing needs, not difficulty ratings or guarantees of competition eligibility.

- **Too technical / Needs context:** explain concepts clearly for readers unfamiliar with the subject.
- **Needs citations / Citation needed / Needs secondary sources:** support claims with reliable sources, including independent reviews where appropriate.
- **Stub / Missing information:** expand limited coverage with well-sourced material.
- Other labels identify specific concerns, such as tone, original research, or notability; read the notice before proposing your edits.
