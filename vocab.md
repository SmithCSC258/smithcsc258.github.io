# Vocabulary

Key terms used throughout the course will be collected here.

| Term | Definition |
|:---|:---|
| **Algorithm** | A finite sequence of steps for solving a problem or performing a computation. |
| **DNA** | Deoxyribonucleic acid, the molecule that stores hereditary information in most living organisms. |
| **Genome** | The complete genetic material of an organism or sample. |
| **k-mer** | A substring of length *k* in a biological sequence. |
| **Origin of replication (ori)** | A location in a genome where DNA replication begins. |
| **Reverse complement** | The sequence formed by reversing a DNA sequence and replacing each nucleotide with its complement. |
